---
title: Frequently asked questions
---

## Can I run the demo on my live site?

It is made for a fresh site you use to show or try the products: a new install, a staging copy or a sandbox. On a
site people already use, the 20 sample people would appear next to your real members in member lists, feeds and
leaderboards until you finish the demo. If the site already has other members, blog posts or comments, the site
check warns you and the build waits until you confirm.

## Are my own users, posts and pages changed?

No. The demo only adds content and records everything it adds. Your users and content are never edited or removed,
and a sample login that is already taken is skipped.

## Does anyone get email?

No. Mail is held while the demo builds, and mail to sample people is never sent, now or later.

## Do I need the Pro add-ons?

No. Every product works on its own and in any mix of free and Pro. When a Pro add-on is active, its extras are
filled in too, for example more activity in the feed.

## What happens to the products' own setup wizards?

The demo finishes each product's setup the way its own wizard does and creates the pages it needs, so you are not
sent to several setup screens. Each wizard is still in its product's menu if you want to run it.

## What is left for me to do after the demo?

The things only the site owner can do: connect a payment method, write your own terms and policies, and set up real
email sending. Some products show a reminder for these, on purpose.

## How do I get my site back?

Use **Finish demo**. "Keep my setup" removes the sample people and their content and keeps pages, menus, spaces,
categories and settings. "Remove everything" also puts back your previous home page, menu and logo.

## Can I run it again?

Yes, once the current demo is finished. After "Keep my setup", choose **Keep them as mine** first; a new build then
reuses the spaces and categories you kept, and adds its own Demo menu without changing yours.

## Does it work with my theme?

It works with any theme. BuddyX, BuddyX Pro and Reign also get the community home page, menu, logo and product
pages without sidebars.

## Do I have to install every product first?

No. The site check lists the free Wbcom products and installs and activates the ones you tick, using the same
releases you get as updates. Pro add-ons need your licence, so install them from your account.
