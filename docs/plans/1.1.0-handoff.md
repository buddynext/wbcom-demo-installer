## Owner decisions (10-11 November 2026)

- **AI is an optional extra, the non-AI path comes first** (owner, 11 November: about 10% of customers will use it).
  Without a connector the wizard reads exactly as without AI: the site check shows no AI, and administrators see one
  quiet tip on Starting point linking to Settings > Connectors. With a connector, one slim "Have a site in mind?" bar
  shows on the site check and on Starting point; the description box opens only on request.
- AI uses only WordPress core's AI connectors (`wp_supports_ai()`, `wp_ai_client_prompt()`); no adapters or key setting
  of our own.
- The owner describes the site in one sentence and gets **3 ideas**; picking one walks the normal steps with
  everything preselected: Starting point shows only the idea's choice ("Choose a different starting point" opens the
  list), Products shows the idea's reason per product, Home page says the header shows the idea's name, Review opens
  with a "Your site" panel. "Use no idea" on any step returns to the normal choices.
- AI rewrites the text for the niche in the site's language; photos come from our CC0 library; no AI images.
- Free products install through the channel each plugin uses for its own updates: the store's EDD endpoint with the
  free key the plugin ships (as BuddyX Pro and Reign do). Product Roadmap and BuddyX come from WordPress.org. Wbcom
  Essential uses its own preset key. Pro stays manual.
- BuddyNext, WPMediaVerse and Jetonomy are one set on every starting point; Wbcom Essential (pages and blog) is part of
  every starting point and every AI idea.
- After "Keep my setup" the setup stays tracked and removable; "Keep them as mine" (`adopt`) hands it over.
- Rows products keep after Remove everything are named in the report, never deleted by us (all checked as kept on
  purpose: WPMediaVerse usage ledger, Learnomy audit log; BuddyNext rate-limit counters expire on their own).
- The installer never edits an owner's menu: the demo adds its own "Demo menu".
- Default taken without asking: a site name typed on Review replaces the sample wordmark logo in the header.
- Usability for customers decides priority (owner, 10 November).

## What the AI builder is (for the next session)

- `src/Ai/Assistant.php`: the one seam to the core AI client; filters `wbcom_demo_ai_available` and
  `wbcom_demo_ai_pre_json` for tests. `src/Ai/Ideas.php`: 3 ideas, checked on the server (only our starting points and
  products). `src/Ai/Pack.php`: every distinct human text of a provider's content file is sent once with an id and the
  photo beside it; the answer becomes a translation map in the build state, applied through `wbcom_demo_content`.
  Slugs, keys, enums, paths, times, links, mention tokens and each provider's `ai_skip_keys()` / `ai_keep()` never
  change; an added hashtag or a broken token rejects that text; a pack under 60% valid keeps the sample text with a
  warning.
- Engine queues `__ai:<n>` steps (one per chunk of about 4,500 characters) before every content step when a brief is
  given; the progress label reads "the text for <product>".
- REST: `POST /ideas`, `/build` with `brief`; WP-CLI: `wp wbcom-demo ideas "..."`, `build --describe="..." --idea=<n>`.
- Known limit: WB Listora listings keep street and city (they match fixed map coordinates).
- Wbcom Essential: `src/Providers/Pages.php` builds About, Join, FAQ and Blog pages from core blocks plus its
  server-rendered post carousel and category grid, and 4 blog posts. Its static blocks (hero, FAQ accordion, feature
  grid) are not written: markup made on the server would show as invalid content in the editor.
