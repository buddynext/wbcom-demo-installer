## Owner decisions (10-11 November 2026)

- AI uses only WordPress core's AI connection (`wp_supports_ai()`, `wp_ai_client_prompt()`); no adapters or key
  setting of our own. The AI option is hidden when core AI is unavailable.
- Free products install through the channel each plugin uses for its own updates: the store's EDD endpoint with the
  free key the plugin ships (as BuddyX Pro and Reign do). Product Roadmap and BuddyX come from WordPress.org. Pro stays
  manual.
- BuddyNext, WPMediaVerse and Jetonomy are one set on every starting point.
- After "Keep my setup" the setup stays tracked and removable; "Keep them as mine" (`adopt`) hands it over.
- Rows products keep after Remove everything are named in the report, never deleted by us. Checked on 11 November:
  all are kept on purpose (WPMediaVerse usage ledger anonymised for accounting, Learnomy audit log for security,
  BuddyNext expired rate-limit counters purged by its own housekeeping), so no product cards were filed.
- The installer never edits an owner's menu: the demo adds its own "Demo menu".
- Default taken without asking: a site name typed on Review replaces the sample wordmark logo in the header.
- Usability for customers decides priority (owner, 10 November).
